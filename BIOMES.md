# Floor Biomes

## Dungeon (Floors 1-3)

**Theme:** Grey stone corridors

| Element | Color | Symbol |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Walls | Grey | `#` |
| Floors | Dark Grey | `.` |
| Stairs | White | `>` |

**Monsters:**
- Goblin (common)
- Bat Swarm (common)
- Spider (uncommon)
- Skeleton (rare)

**Item Tier:** 1 (basic)

**Difficulty:** Tutorial. Learn combat basics.

## Boss Floors

| Floor | Boss | Special Mechanics |
|-------|------|-------------------|
| 5 | Goblin King | Summons minions, enrages |
| 10 | Bone Dragon | Breath attack, slow |
| 15 | Shadow Lord | Teleport, drain, pulse |

**Boss Rules:**
- Stairs sealed until boss defeated
- Boss announcement on floor entry
- Boss drops guaranteed high-tier loot
- Boss spawns near stairs
